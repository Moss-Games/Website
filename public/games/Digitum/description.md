**Step into a magical adventure like no other!**

Have you ever wondered what it would be like to be a rock, an olive, or a small, perfectly rollable sphere? Ever dreamed of racing downhill at breakneck speed, just to go faster than your friends and claim victory? Look no further—**welcome to Digitum!**

Here, you take on the role of an old, used magician, pushing objects through finger-sized portals simply because… well, you're bored and you can.

As the player, you control one of these curious little objects, guiding it from portal to portal in a never-ending game of whimsical physics and chaotic fun, trying to improve the time you take to finish a level!

**The goal of the game is to complete each level as quickly as possible while avoiding obstacles that add time to your timer.**

To move forward, you give an impulse to the object you control. A loading bar displayed beneath the object indicates the strength of the impulse. If the bar turns red, an additional boost is triggered, making you go even faster.

The game features a multiplayer mode with a real-time leaderboard showing the scores of your friends and the top players.

Many successes can be unlocked by completing challenges — some of which are hidden — and allow you to obtain new skins for your object.
